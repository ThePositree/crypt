## Pocket Field Lab — PRIMARY

SOURCE:
- `../visual-directions/03-pocket-field-lab.html`

MODEL/TOOL AND DATE:
- Native Codex visual worker, coordinator QA through Orca `orca-ide`, and
  independent Cursor Grok 4.6 High Fast review; 2026-08-30.

LIKE:
- Tactile field-notebook hierarchy, specimen-led chapters, index tabs, taped
  diagrams, compact labels, dusty pastel palette, and strong mobile trail.
- Human expedition-documentarian guide who helps explain evidence and system
  boundaries.
- Moderate density that supports both sequential reading and quick scanning.

AVOID:
- Scrapbook clutter, childish school-project styling, fake paper textures that
  reduce contrast, excessive rotations, and decoration competing with exact
  charts or code examples.

DO NOT COPY:
- The exploration board's literal page copy, one-off layout dimensions, or
  embedded prototype implementation as production components.

LOCAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE:
- Technical evidence should feel collected, labelled, and inspectable. The
  friendly field-guide metaphor must never obscure causal rules, source dates,
  limitations, or the research/live boundary.

APPROVAL:
- approved as primary by the owner on 2026-08-30.
