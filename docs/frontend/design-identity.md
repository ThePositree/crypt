# Design Identity

Status: approved.
Revision: 2.
Approval source: owner selected Pocket Field Lab on 2026-08-30 after reviewing
five rendered visual direction boards.

## Core Feeling

- Decision: curious, welcoming, and quietly technical rather than intimidating
  or financially aggressive.
- Evidence: the owner selected a cartoon lo-fi laboratory in pastel colors and
  requested explanations accessible to a broad public audience.

## Personality

- Decision: an enthusiastic human researcher who makes complex systems feel
  explorable, honest, and safe to learn.
- Evidence: the owner selected a recurring named human mascot and allowed
  playful interactions.

## Desired Perception

- Decision: a memorable educational research portal whose technical evidence
  remains credible beneath its friendly presentation.
- Evidence: success requires visual impact, architectural clarity, content
  completeness, and public-launch readiness together.

## Visual Tension

- Decision: cozy hand-drawn laboratory atmosphere paired with exact charts,
  explicit system boundaries, and disciplined technical hierarchy.
- Evidence: the owner requested stylized charts while preserving exact values,
  visible crypto motifs, both light and dark themes, and moderate density.

## Signature Traits

- Trait: recurring named human laboratory guide.
- Product purpose: provides continuity, orientation, and plain-language help.

- Trait: animated crypto research apparatus and data-flow paths.
- Product purpose: makes architecture visible and understandable; static
  reduced-motion alternatives preserve the same information.

- Trait: pastel lo-fi charts with precise axes, labels, dates, and methodology.
- Product purpose: joins the playful identity to trustworthy evidence.

- Trait: immediate crypto cues integrated into the laboratory environment.
- Product purpose: communicates domain at a glance without imitating an
  exchange dashboard.

- Trait: tactile field-notebook structure with specimen cards, index tabs,
  taped diagrams, clipped samples, and restrained stamped labels.
- Product purpose: makes a large technical system feel browsable and collected
  through evidence rather than presented as a generic documentation template.

- Trait: vertically browsable research specimens with an intentionally guided
  single-column mobile trail.
- Product purpose: preserves equal mobile and desktop quality while supporting
  both sequential learning and quick retrieval.

- Trait: dusty mint, warm sand, muted coral, and faded ink as the primary
  palette, with a coordinated dark-paper theme.
- Product purpose: delivers the approved pastel lo-fi character while keeping
  charts, code, states, and body text technically legible.

## Anti-Identity

- Avoid: dark fintech terminal clichés, neon casino styling, luxury trading
  imagery, profit hype, and exchange-dashboard composition.
- Reason: these imply operational trading, exclusivity, or promised returns.

- Avoid: childish simplification that removes evidence or technical truth.
- Reason: accessibility must come from explanation and hierarchy, not from
  hiding complexity.

- Avoid: animation that blocks reading, obscures charts, or ignores reduced
  motion.
- Reason: motion supports comprehension and atmosphere; it is not the product.

- Avoid: GitHub-centric navigation and unexplained source excerpts.
- Reason: the portal is self-contained and teaches primarily through
  pseudocode.
