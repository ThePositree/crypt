The orbital system map

One decision core,
two explicit paths.

Research instruments orbit shared pure strategy logic. Only closed candles enter decisions. Backtests simulate outcomes; the separately configured live path may execute the owner-selected strategy on OKX.

01

Causal by construction

Indicators and features use closed candles only, preventing future information from leaking backward into a decision.

02

Evidence, not permission

The strategy benchmark is a reporting target—not a production gate. Production selection belongs to the owner.

When the sky is incomplete

Missing data must produce a neutral signal, block an entry, or surface an explicit operator error. It is never silently invented.

Board notes

Night Shift Observatory

Hypothesis

A quiet celestial lab can make causal data flow memorable without borrowing the visual language of trading terminals.

Product rationale

Orbit diagrams connect research instruments to one decision core and distinguish simulation from owner-selected OKX execution.

Composition

Editorial hero, radial system map, evidence pairing, state specimens, glossary, then rationale. Mobile becomes a single guided chapter stream.

Hierarchy

Large serif chapter titles anchor scanning; mint eyebrows, exact labels, and concise prose resolve detail.

Typography

System sans for accessible UI and Georgia for observatory-scale editorial headings; no remote font dependency.

Density

Moderate: spacious narrative openings alternate with compact evidence tables and code.

Geometry

Orbits, circular instruments, softly clipped cards, and thin technical lines. No hard terminal grid.

Surfaces

Layered navy and muted plum create night-sky depth; panels remain opaque enough for comfortable reading.

Color

Pastel lavender, mint, peach, gold, and rose carry meaning. The theme control proves a warm daylight counterpart.

Iconography

Simple typographic marks and inline SVG keep the language hand-made, self-contained, and non-financial.

Motion

Slow orbital drift and data-path breathing suggest observation. Reduced-motion removes all animation while preserving structure.

Intentionally excluded

No trading controls, account data, live commands, exchange dashboard, GitHub dependency, performance promise, neon cyberpunk, or casino energy.

Inspected targets

Designed for 1440×1000, 390×844, and 1728×1117. Coordinator-rendered QA remains required before approval.
