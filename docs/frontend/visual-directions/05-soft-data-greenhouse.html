Glossary propagation bench

Terms, without thorns

Definitions are short at the point of need and lead to deeper concept pages.

Closed candle

Complete OHLCV data for a finished interval; safe for causal decisions.

Backtest

A historical simulation used to study strategy behavior under stated assumptions.

Parity

Agreement between shared backtest and live decision behavior at the same boundary.

Neutral signal

No new directional action, including when required evidence is unavailable.

Benchmark

A reporting target for evidence comparison, not a gate on owner production choice.

Owner-selected path

The configured strategy promoted by the owner to the separate OKX execution module.

Board notes

Why this direction exists

A visual hypothesis for review—not production code, approved identity, or a final information architecture.

Hypothesis + rationale

An organic greenhouse makes a complex research pipeline feel learnable. Cultivation suggests care, iteration, and evidence without implying profit.

Composition + hierarchy

Editorial hero, labeled ecosystem map, then evidence and learning modules. Mira orients; technical labels carry authority.

Typography + density

Georgia brings humane editorial character; system sans keeps data exact. Moderate density, generous section rhythm, compact evidence panels.

Geometry + surfaces

Rounded botanical silhouettes, transparent pipe lines, seed-tray modules, translucent glass panels, and grounded borders—not arbitrary blobs.

Color + iconography

Soft green foundation with butter, lilac, and sky roles. Simple line symbols are semantic station markers; no external icon set.

Motion

Three data dots demonstrate direction of flow. Reduced-motion freezes them at useful positions and removes all nonessential transitions.

Mobile composition

At 390×844 the hero stacks, controls simplify, system stations become a swipeable field guide, tables remain compact, and secondary glossary entries trim.

Intentionally excluded

No trading controls, account data, live commands, GitHub dependency, profit claims, exchange-dashboard UI, generic wellness tropes, or unrelated plants.

Inspected targets

Designed for coordinator inspection at 1440×1000, 390×844, and 1728×1117. Rendered QA remains intentionally with the coordinator.
