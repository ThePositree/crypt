Pocket glossary

Terms pinned where they are needed

Definitions orient the reader; they never hide required explanations.
Closed candle
A completed market interval whose final open, high, low, close, and volume values are known.
Backtest
A historical replay used to inspect how decision logic would have behaved.
Parity
Agreement between shared decision behavior in research and live paths under equivalent inputs.
Benchmark
A reporting target for evidence comparison, not an automatic production gate.

Board notes

Direction hypothesis & design decisions

not production UI
  • Hypothesis: a disciplined field notebook makes dense architecture feel explorable while retaining evidence.
  • Product rationale: sequential specimens support guided learning and answer retrieval without dashboard cues.
  • Composition: sticky utility header, oversized overview, then a vertical archive of numbered specimens.
  • Hierarchy: bold editorial headings, mono specimen labels, concise evidence annotations.
  • Typography: system rounded sans plus system monospace; no remote font dependency.
  • Density: compact but moderate, with one primary teaching idea per specimen.
  • Geometry: clipped tabs, gentle offsets, ruled structure, and restrained tape motifs.
  • Surfaces: flat paper layers and crisp borders; no faux-realistic texture that harms legibility.
  • Color: dusty mint, sand, coral, and faded ink, with paired light/dark tokens.
  • Iconography: simple text glyphs and inline SVG; crypto cues support orientation, not trading.
  • Motion: quiet flow pulses, chart reveal, and loading sweep; all removed under reduced motion.
  • Mobile: single-column expedition trail, mascot introduced early, sticky trail label, horizontally scrollable exact chart/table.
  • Intentionally excluded: trading controls, account data, live commands, exchange dashboard patterns, profit claims, GitHub dependency, scrapbook clutter, childish school styling.
  • Inspected targets for coordinator QA: 1440×1000 desktop, 390×844 mobile, and 1728×1117 wide desktop.
