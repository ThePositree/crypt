# Troubleshooting

- Route: `/docs/troubleshooting`.
- Purpose: map observable symptoms to causes, safe recovery, and verification.
- Primary action: select/search a symptom and follow its recovery checklist.
- Hierarchy: search; environment/install; missing/corrupt data; backtester;
  configuration; OKX/sync; runtime; Railway/storage/logging; escalation evidence.
- Messaging: from `Something failed` to `I know the boundary and verification step`.
- Content contract: only reproduced/source-backed failures; no destructive shortcut.
- Discovery: error phrases, symptoms, commands, settings and recovery vocabulary.
- Interactions: symptom search/filter, accordion checklists, copy, anchors, related guides.
- States: results, zero results, unresolved issue, blocked unsafe recovery, copy failure.
- Responsive: checklist and evidence blocks stay ordered; tables become labeled rows.
- Accessibility: accordions expose state; error icons supplement explicit labels.
- Related: Search flow and every operational guide.
- Acceptance: each entry contains symptom, likely cause, safe action, and verification.
