# Glossary

## Purpose

Provide an alphabetic concept reference connected to portal sections.

## Primary Action

Find a term and follow related docs.

## Information Hierarchy

1. Search/filter controls.
2. Alphabet index.
3. Term list.
4. Selected definition and related links.

## Messaging Contract

- Main idea: glossary resolves local project vocabulary and links each concept
  back to the system.
- Required proof: definitions, relationships, and section labels.
- Objections: terms are curated for portal understanding, not generic crypto
  education.

## Content And Capability Contract

Coverage includes core concepts such as signal, candle, strategy, engine,
router, risk, sink, backtester, OKX, execution, parity, benchmark, and runtime
truth.

## Discovery Contract

Term search, alphabet filter, section filter, related concept links, zero-term
state.

## Interaction Inventory

Search, alphabet buttons, section filters, term rows, related links, keyboard
navigation.

## States

empty query, filtered terms, selected term, zero-result, overflow definition,
dark theme.

## Acceptance Criteria

Every major page links to relevant glossary terms and glossary entries link
back to pages.
