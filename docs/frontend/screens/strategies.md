# Strategies

## Purpose

Explain how strategy logic, engines, filters, routing, and candidate archives
fit together.

## Primary Action

Understand a strategy lifecycle or open an extension recipe.

## Information Hierarchy

1. Strategy mental model.
2. Signal generation and filters.
3. Portfolio/router composition.
4. Candidate and archive boundaries.
5. Add-or-extend recipes.

## Messaging Contract

- Main idea: strategies are curated rule systems that should be testable and
  share decision behavior with backtest/live paths where possible.
- Required proof: lifecycle and contract blocks.
- Objections: benchmark is a reporting target, not a production veto.

## Content And Capability Contract

Coverage includes engines, strategies, discovery, filters, routers, candidate
archives, benchmark role, and owner production override.

## Interaction Inventory

Lifecycle tabs, recipe cards, glossary links, related architecture links.

## States

normal, selected route, expanded recipe, disabled future integration note,
dark theme.

## Acceptance Criteria

The page distinguishes strategy evidence from owner-selected production truth.
